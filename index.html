热

2026-07-13

在2026白昼最长的一天，晚上九点的天空就像十七岁的下午，悠长而安静。当然十七岁的下午并不如此安静，是湿热聒噪的，头昏脑胀的只剩下纸笔摩擦的声音。似乎所有中国人的生命被十八岁的高考刻上了一个非常深的烙印，把你的生命切割成两半。那时最远只能遥望十八岁时高考的这堵高墙，只是翻越这座高墙就已经让人难以喘息，至于高墙背后是什么已无暇顾及。

I no longer remember exactly how the film ended. It simply faded away quietly, ending as calmly as the story itself had unfolded. It felt as though the final scene could have been placed at almost any moment during the last half hour and it would not have seemed out of place. There were no dramatic separations, no grand tragedies — it was quiet, almost as if nothing had really been said.

This kind of quietness is like a breeze that passes through everyone’s life. Later, when you look back, you may not remember exactly what happened that day. But the feeling of that day, the song you heard, the smell in the air — they will occasionally bring you back to that moment. Your nose may sting slightly, your eyes may become a little wet, but not enough to squeeze out even a single tear.
